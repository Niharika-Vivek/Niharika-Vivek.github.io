SMALL GOOGLE APPS SCRIPT LIMIT UPDATE
The original supplied receiver allows only 12 adults and 12 children. The new interface accepts at most TWO digits and a maximum TOTAL of 99 guests. To match this:
1. Google Sheet > Extensions > Apps Script.
2. In the validation line change adults>12 to adults>99.
3. Change children>12 to children>99||adults+children>99.
4. Do NOT change SPREADSHEET_ID or any other settings. Save.
5. Deploy > Manage deployments > select your existing Web app > Edit (pencil) > Version: New version > Deploy.
6. Keep the same existing deployment; this retains the /exec URL, so config.js remains unchanged. If Google gives you a different URL because you created a new deployment instead, update config.js with that new /exec URL.
7. If your existing script already uses 99 limits, do not apply the replacements again.
